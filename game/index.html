THE RITES — HOW IT SCORES

Each courtyard pays 1,000 for the crossing, up to +10 per second under its par, and +250 per shade crystal — all multiplied by your season. Burnings cost you time, never score directly, but they stain your grade.

Grades run D to S. Feats are kept forever: cross untouched, hoard crystals, keep the Dusk flawless, walk in Autumn, finish swifter than light.

The shade you stand in is real: every pillar, tree, statue and wall throws it, and the sun you aim decides where it lies. If a road looks impossible, turn the sun — not yourself.
